[NORMAL_TEXT] H2: Clean Water, Gray Water or Sewage: Why the Water Type Matters
[NORMAL_TEXT] The IICRC S500 standard sorts water into three categories, and the category decides how the job is done.
[NORMAL_TEXT] • Category 1 (clean water): a broken supply line or a leaking water heater. Most materials can be dried if work starts fast.
[NORMAL_TEXT] • Category 2 (gray water): washing machine overflow, dishwasher discharge or water that has picked up contamination. More materials need to come out.
[NORMAL_TEXT] • Category 3 (black water): sewage, floor drain backups during storms and outside floodwater. Porous materials like carpet, pad and drywall are removed, and everything is disinfected.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Clean water does not stay clean. After a day or two in a warm basement, Category 1 water can turn into Category 2 or 3. That is one more reason to call early. Read more in our guide to Category 1, 2 and 3 water (/blog/category-1-2-3-water-damage/).
