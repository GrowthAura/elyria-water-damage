Frozen Fire Sprinkler Pipe Bursts

One of the most common winter commercial losses in Northeast Ohio is a frozen fire sprinkler pipe. Lines in unheated stairwells, loading docks, above drop ceilings near exterior walls or in vacant suites can freeze and burst, dumping large amounts of water quickly. These losses often soak ceiling tiles, insulation, drywall and inventory over several floors. Fast response limits how far the water travels. Your fire protection contractor repairs the system, and we handle extraction, removal and drying.
