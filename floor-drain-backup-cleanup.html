Why Is Water Coming Up Through Basement Floor Drains in Elyria?

During heavy rain, sewer and storm lines can fill and push water backward into the lowest drain in the house. A blocked sewer lateral can do the same without rain.

Many Elyria basements have a floor drain near the furnace, water heater or laundry that ties into the home's sewer line. When the line downstream is full or blocked, water has to go somewhere, and the floor drain is usually the lowest opening. You may see clear water at first, then dark water, debris and a sewer smell.

Is Floor Drain Water Stormwater or Sewage?

Assume it is contaminated. Even when it looks clear, water coming up a floor drain often mixes with sewage from the same line. Under the IICRC S500 standard it is treated as Category 3 water. That means carpet, pad, drywall and other porous materials it touched are removed, and everything else is cleaned and disinfected. For a full sewage cleanup, see sewage backup cleanup.

Plumber vs Restoration Crew: Who Does What?

  • Plumber or drain service: clears the blockage, camera-inspects the lateral and repairs or replaces pipe.
  • Restoration crew: removes the water, takes out contaminated materials, disinfects and dries the basement.
  • The city: handles problems in the public sewer main.

Often you need both a drain service and a cleanup crew. If you are unsure who to call first, read restoration company or plumber.

Our Floor Drain Backup Cleanup Process

Step 1. Stop using water in the house so the backup does not grow.

Step 2. Extract water and debris with pumps and extraction tools.

Step 3. Remove porous materials that the water touched.

Step 4. Clean and disinfect floors, walls, framing and hard contents.

Step 5. Dry with dehumidifiers and air movers.

Step 6. Deodorize with HEPA filtration so the sewer smell does not linger.

Floor Drain Sewage Smell After Cleanup

A sewer smell from a floor drain when there is no backup usually means the drain trap has dried out. Pour a gallon of water into the drain every few weeks to keep the trap sealed. If the smell continues after cleanup, the drain line may be cracked or the backup soaked into materials that were not removed.

Backwater Valves: Stopping Repeat Backups

A backwater valve installed on your main sewer line lets water leave the house but closes when water tries to flow back in. For homes that back up during storms, it is one of the most effective upgrades. A plumber installs it, and some cities offer programs to help with the cost, so ask the City of Elyria about any current options.

Floor Drain Backing Up Elyria Homes: Storm vs Blockage

There are two main reasons a floor drain backs up in Elyria:

  • Storm surge: during heavy rain, sewer lines fill and water flows backward into basement drains. Often affects several homes on the same street at once.
  • Private line blockage: roots, grease, wipes or a broken lateral block your own line. Usually happens when you run water, like doing laundry, and affects only your home.

Knowing which one it is helps you call the right people. Storm backups may be reported to the City of Elyria. Private blockages need a drain service.

Basement Drain Overflow Cleanup Elyria: What Happens to Contents

Items on the floor during a drain backup are often damaged. Hard items like metal shelving, tools and plastic bins can be cleaned and disinfected. Cardboard, paper, fabric and upholstered items that touched the water are usually discarded. We photograph and list everything for your insurance claim before removal.

Laundry Rooms and Floor Drains

In many Elyria homes, the washing machine drains into a laundry tub or standpipe near the floor drain. If the main line is partly blocked, the washer's discharge can back up through the floor drain. Gray water from the washer mixes with sewage from the line. Stop using the washer until the line is cleared.

Floor Drain Backups in Finished Basements

Finished basements suffer the most from drain backups because drywall, carpet and pad soak up contaminated water. Flood cuts, pad and carpet removal, disinfection and drying are usually needed. See finished basement water damage.

Preventing Floor Drain Backups

  • Install a backwater valve on the main line
  • Use a floor drain plug or check valve made for basement drains
  • Have the sewer lateral camera-inspected and cleared of roots
  • Never pour grease down drains or flush wipes
  • Avoid running laundry or dishwashers during heavy storms if your drain has backed up before

Water coming up from a floor drain is one of the clearest signs your home needs one of these upgrades.

Water Coming Up From Floor Drain: What It Looks Like

Backups often start slowly: a little water around the drain grate, then a growing puddle. Storm backups can rise quickly and spread across the whole basement floor. The water may look clear at first and turn darker, with debris, as the backup continues. A sewer smell is a strong sign the water is mixed with sewage.

Floor Drain Backups in Older Elyria Homes

Older Elyria homes often have floor drains tied directly into the house's main sewer line, plus clay tile laterals running to the street. Over decades, roots enter the joints and slow the flow. During storms, high flow in the city system adds pressure. Both together make these homes more likely to back up. A camera inspection shows the condition of the lateral and whether roots or cracks are a problem.

Backflow Valve Basement Options

  • Mainline backwater valve: installed on the main sewer line; protects every drain in the house.
  • Floor drain check valve: a simple insert in the floor drain that lets water out but blocks it from coming up.
  • Floor drain plug: a mechanical plug for drains that are not used.

A plumber can recommend the right option for your home and confirm that it meets local code.

Basement Drain Overflow Cleanup: How Long It Takes

Most floor drain backup cleanups take one to two days for removal and disinfection, then three to five days of drying. Finished basements take longer because of drywall and flooring removal. Rebuild happens after drying is confirmed.

Reporting a Storm Backup to the City

If several homes on your street had water come up through floor drains during the same storm, the cause may be in the public sewer system. Report it to the City of Elyria. This does not replace cleanup, but it helps the city track problem areas and may matter for your records.

Floor Drain Backups and Insurance

Most homeowners policies exclude backup damage unless you have a water backup endorsement. Even with the endorsement, limits apply. Clear photos of water at the drain and notes on the storm help classify the claim correctly. See does homeowners insurance cover sewer backup in Ohio .

Why Fast Cleanup Matters

Contaminated water soaks into concrete, drywall and wood within hours. Bacteria multiply and odors set in. Starting cleanup the same day limits how much material must be removed.

Floor Drain Backup FAQs

Why is water coming up through my floor drain?

The sewer or storm line is full or blocked, so water flows backward into the lowest drain in your home.

Is floor drain water sewage?

Treat it as contaminated. Water from a floor drain often mixes with sewage in the same line.

Should I keep using water during a backup?

No. Stop running water, flushing toilets and using appliances until the line is clear.

Can I clean it up with bleach?

Bleach does not reach water absorbed into drywall, wood and carpet backing. Contaminated porous materials need to be removed.
