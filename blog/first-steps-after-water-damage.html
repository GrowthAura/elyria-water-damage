What Is the Very First Thing To Do After Water Damage?

Make it safe and stop the water: shut off the main water valve, keep away from water near electricity and turn off power to wet areas only if you can do it from a dry spot.

The 10 Steps

1. Stay safe

Do not step into water near outlets, cords or appliances. If you smell gas, leave and call Columbia Gas of Ohio.

2. Stop the water

Close the main water valve, usually in the basement near the meter on the street side.

3. Cut the power to wet areas

Use the breaker panel only if you can reach it without standing in water.

4. Figure out what kind of water it is

Clean water from a pipe is different from storm water or sewage. Stay out of anything that could be sewage.

5. Take photos and video

Capture the source, the water and every affected room before moving things.

6. Move valuables

Get photos, documents, electronics and rugs to a dry floor.

7. Call a water damage crew

Drying is time-sensitive. A crew can extract water and start drying the same day.

8. Call your insurance company

Report the loss the same day and note the claim number.

9. Keep damaged parts and receipts

Save the burst hose or failed valve, and keep receipts for any emergency costs.

10. Do not rush into repairs

New drywall or flooring over wet framing traps moisture and grows mold. Repairs come after drying is confirmed.

First Steps by Water Source

Burst pipe

Shut the main valve, open the lowest faucet to drain lines, turn off power to wet areas and move items out of the way.

Sump pump failure

Check for a power outage or tripped breaker only if you can reach it safely, move items upstairs and call for pump-out.

Floor drain or sewage backup

Stop using all water in the house, stay out of the water and keep children and pets away.

Ice dam leak

Put buckets under drips, turn off power to wet fixtures and clear snow from the lower roof with a roof rake from the ground.

Water heater leak

Close the cold water valve on top, turn off gas or power to the unit and keep clear of hot water.

Appliance leak

Shut the valves behind the washer, under the sink or behind the fridge, and unplug the appliance if it is safe.

What To Do When Your Basement Floods in Elyria

Basements are the most common place for water damage in Elyria homes. After a storm, assume basement water is contaminated. Do not walk through it if power is still on, and do not try to bail it out by hand. Move what you can from the stairs, photograph everything and call for help. If you have a sump pump, check whether it is running only if you can do so safely. See flooded basement cleanup.

How Long Do You Have Before Mold?

Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours on wet materials. That is the window that matters most. Even if the water looks small, call for a moisture check if it soaked carpet, drywall or wood. See how fast mold grows after a basement flood.

Who To Call First

If the water is still flowing and you cannot stop it, call a plumber or shut the main valve. Once water is stopped, call a restoration crew to start drying. If sewage is involved, stay out of the water and call for Category 3 cleanup. Read restoration company or plumber.

Keeping Your Family Safe

  • Keep children and pets away from wet areas
  • Do not eat food that touched floodwater or sewage
  • Wear gloves and boots if you must go near water
  • Wash hands well after touching anything wet
  • Leave if you smell gas, and call Columbia Gas of Ohio from outside

Safety comes before belongings every time.

Water Damage Checklist to Save on Your Phone

Water off. Power off in wet areas. Photos taken. Valuables moved. Crew called. Insurer called. Receipts saved. Keep this list handy so you are not trying to remember steps during a stressful moment.

After the Crew Arrives

The crew will check safety, confirm the source is stopped, extract water and map moisture with meters and thermal imaging. They will explain what can be dried and what must be removed, set up drying equipment and return daily to take readings. Ask questions at each step. You should understand what is being done and why.

First Steps for Renters

If you rent, stop the water if you can, tell your landlord right away in writing and photograph your belongings. Call your renters insurance company about damaged items. If water is spreading and the landlord cannot be reached, call for emergency help.

What Not To Do

  • Do not use a household vacuum on water
  • Do not turn on ceiling lights or fans under a wet ceiling
  • Do not walk through sewage or floodwater
  • Do not paint over stains before the area is dry
  • Do not throw out damaged items before photographing them

When To Call a Crew

Call right away if water is standing, spreading, came through a ceiling, came up a floor drain or soaked carpet.
